About GAPHS

The Global Association of Professional Home Stagers.

GAPHS is a non-profit association registered in Switzerland and a global movement of professionals, educators and partners committed to elevating the standards of Home Staging across continents, cultures and markets.

Our mission

To advance and elevate the profession of Home Staging worldwide by setting and upholding high standards of Education, Ethics and Excellence, and by supporting professionals through certification, collaboration and continued development.

Our vision

For Home Staging to be universally recognised as an essential global profession and an indispensable part of the real estate sector, with GAPHS setting the benchmarks, supporting professionals and shaping policy and public perception.

Our commitment to education

Education is the foundation of professional integrity. Every GAPHS Member has completed formal training in Home Staging, ensuring a consistent benchmark of excellence across the profession. Through education, global collaboration and ongoing support, we equip professionals with the tools, recognition and network they need to succeed in an evolving industry.

National chapters

Representing the profession nationally.

GAPHS National Chapters represent the Association in their country and bring members together in their own language. They organise educational and networking events, promote Home Staging with local media and real estate agencies, and take part in GAPHS-wide campaigns and conferences.

GAPHS Italia was founded in Rome in April 2026. Every chapter operates under the governance of GAPHS and to the same standards and Code of Ethics.

Start a chapter

A minimum of five active members in a country may submit a Chapter Foundation Proposal to the Board. Upon approval, a chapter receives:

  • Official recognition and its own chapter logo
  • An announcement on every GAPHS channel
  • Mentoring from the Board and established chapter leaders
  • Promotion of its local and national events
