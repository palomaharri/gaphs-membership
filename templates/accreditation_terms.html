Accreditation Terms

These terms apply to applications for, and holding, GAPHS Accredited membership. They apply in addition to the GAPHS Terms and Conditions and Code of Conduct. "GAPHS", "we" and "us" mea GAPHS. "You" means the member or applicant.

1. Accredited membership

1.1 Accredited membership is a Professional membership with the addition of Accredited status, which we award after assessing your application.

1.2 By submitting an application you confirm that you have read and accept these terms, and that everything you submit is accurate and is your own company's work.

2. Fee

2.1 The fee for Accredited membership is €500 a year, payable in advance when you apply and on each renewal.

2.2 The fee covers the assessment of your application and your membership for the year.

2.3 The fee is non-refundable in all circumstances. This includes where we ask you to revise your application, where you withdraw it, where Accredited status is not awarded, and where Accredited status is later suspended or withdrawn.

2.4 Upgrading. If you are already a Member or Professional member, you may apply for Accredited membership at any time. An upgrade is a full new application and starts a new membership year. The full Accredited fee is payable. You do not pay only the difference between the two memberships, and no credit, discount or refund is given for the unused part of your existing membership.

3. Your application

3.1 You must provide links to at least three portfolio projects, your home staging course certificates, and the other information requested on the application form.

3.2 We may ask you for further information or evidence at any point during the assessment.

4. Assessment and outcome

4.1 Assessment begins once we have received your application and your payment. We aim to review applications within 10 days.

4.2 The outcome of an assessment is one of the following: (a) Accredited status is awarded; or (b) we send you written feedback explaining what we would like to see before Accredited status can be awarded.

4.3 Feedback is given with the intention of helping you reach Accredited status. You have 90 days from the date of our feedback to update and resubmit your application, at no further charge. We aim to review a resubmitted application within 10 days.

4.4 While your application is being assessed or revised, you hold Professional membership and all of its benefits.

4.5 If you do not resubmit within 90 days, or your application still does not meet the standard, you remain a Professional member for the rest of your membership year. No refund or part refund is due. You may apply again at your next renewal.

4.6 Our decision on any application is final. We are not obliged to give reasons beyond the feedback we provide.

5. Term and renewal

5.1 Accredited status runs with your membership year and renews when you pay the Accredited fee for the following year.

5.2 We may ask for an updated portfolio or other evidence at renewal, or at any other time, to confirm that the standard is still being met.

6. Using your Accredited status

6.1 You may describe your company as GAPHS Accredited, and use the GAPHS Accredited logo, only once Accredited status has been awarded and only while it is current.

6.2 You must not announce or advertise an application, an expected result, or an intention to become Accredited.

6.3 If your Accredited status or your membership ends, is suspended or is withdrawn, you must stop using the Accredited name and logo immediately and remove them from your website, social media, marketing materials and email signatures. The provisions of GAPHS Terms and Conditions on use of GAPHS name and logo, including any charges, apply.

7. Suspension and withdrawal

7.1 We may suspend or withdraw Accredited status if you breach these terms, the GAPHS Terms and Conditions or the Code of Conduct; if information you gave us was inaccurate or misleading; if we receive complaints about your work or conduct; or if you do not respond to a request under clause 5.2.

7.2 No refund is due if Accredited status is suspended or withdrawn.

8. Changes to your business

8.1 You must tell us about significant changes to your business, such as a change of ownership or of the services you offer.

9. Responsibility

9.1 Accredited status reflects our assessment of the material you submitted. It is not a guarantee of your work, and we accept no responsibility for the services you provide to your clients or for any claim made against you.

10. Your information

10.1 We keep your application and the material you submit for as long as needed to assess it and for as long as you hold Accredited status, in line with our Privacy Policy.

11. General

11.1 We may update these terms from time to time. The current version is the one published on this page.

11.2 These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
