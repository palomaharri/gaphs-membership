Apply for Accredited How it works

Stand out

Anyone can call themselves a home stager. Accredited status tells clients, estate agents and developers that your company has been looked at closely by the industry's association: your portfolio, your training and your track record.

It is the highest level of GAPHS membership, designed to set experienced companies apart from the rest of the market.

Who it is for

  • Home staging companies that are already trading
  • With a portfolio of completed projects to show
  • And formal home staging training behind them
  • Who want a mark that sets them apart when clients compare

What you get

  • Everything in Professional membership
  • GAPHS Accredited status, awarded after assessment
  • The GAPHS Accredited logo for your website and marketing
  • An Accredited mark on your directory page

What you need to apply

Have these ready before you start. Everything is sent as links, so there are no large files to upload.

  • Portfolio. Links to at least three completed projects: pages on your website, or a shared folder (Google Drive, Dropbox). Before and after photos are ideal.
  • Course certificates. A shared link to each home staging course certificate you hold.
  • Your experience. How long your company has been trading and roughly how many projects you have completed.
  • Your business details. The same details as any Professional member: they become your directory page.
  • Anything else that supports your application (optional): awards, press, insurance, team, areas covered.

How it works

  1. Apply. Fill in the application, agree to the Accreditation Terms and pay the yearly fee.
  2. You are a member straight away. Your Professional membership and directory page are active from the day you pay.
  3. We review. Your application goes under review once payment is received. We aim to reply within 10 days.
  4. The outcome. We confirm your Accredited status, or we send you feedback on what we would like to see, with the aim of getting you there.
  5. If we send feedback. You have 90 days to update your application from your account, at no extra cost, and we review it again.
  6. Once accredited. Your account shows Accredited, the mark appears on your directory page, and your Accredited logo is ready to download.

Fee and renewal

  • €500 a year, paid when you apply and on each renewal.
  • The fee covers your assessment and your membership for the year.
  • It is non-refundable, including while an application is being revised.
  • Accredited status runs with your membership year and renews with it.

Already a member? You can upgrade at any time. It is a full new application at the full €500 fee: nothing is credited from your current membership, and you do not pay just the difference.

Questions

What happens if my application is not accepted straight away?

We send you written feedback and you have 90 days to update your application at no extra cost. In the meantime you keep your Professional membership and directory page.

Can I say I am Accredited while my application is under review?

No. You can use the Accredited name and logo only once your status has been confirmed, and while it is current.

Do I have to send my photos?

No files, just links: to your website portfolio or to a shared folder.

Is the fee refundable if I change my mind?

No. The fee covers the assessment and your membership year, and is non-refundable in all cases.

Where are the full terms?

In the Accreditation Terms. You will be asked to agree to them when you apply.

Ready to apply?

The application takes about ten minutes if you have your links to hand.
