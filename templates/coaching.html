About coaching

Courses teach you the method. Coaching looks at your business. In a one-to-one session with Paloma, you bring the question that is in front of you right now and work through it with someone who has built, grown and led home staging businesses, and the association itself.

It is practical and specific to you: your market, your clients, your numbers and your next step.

What you can work on

  • Starting out: your services, your first clients and what to charge
  • Pricing and proposals that protect your margin
  • Winning work from estate agents, developers and property investors
  • Growing: stock, a team and the systems behind a busy staging company
  • Your brand, your portfolio and how you present your work
  • A project, pitch or decision you would like a second opinion on

Who it is for

  • New home stagers who want a clear plan
  • Established stagers ready to grow or reposition
  • Interior designers adding staging to their services

What to expect

  • A private session, one to one with Paloma
  • Your business and your questions set the agenda
  • Clear, practical next steps to take away
  • Available to GAPHS members

Your coach

Paloma Harrington is the founder of GAPHS, an award-winning interior designer and home staging specialist. She is Vice President of GAPHS, the Global Association of Professional Home Stagers, and a former Global Director of the International Association of Home Staging Professionals®. She has run her own businesses since the age of 25, across staging, interior design and renovation, and has spoken to audiences of more than 5,000 people across the UK, USA, Spain, Brazil and Dubai.
