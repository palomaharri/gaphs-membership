Questions

Do I need design or staging experience?

No. Every course starts from the beginning and explains each term and step.

Will it work in my country?

Yes. The method is designed to work worldwide, and the lessons show how to apply it in your market, including with international buyers.

How do the lessons work?

Each course is a set of narrated modules you can follow at your own pace on any device, with downloadable workbooks, checklists and templates.

I am a real estate agent. Which course is for me?

Home Staging for Real Estate Agents. It shows you how to use staging as a marketing tool in your listings and how to partner with professional stagers.

Which course should I start with?

If you are unsure, begin with Introduction to Home Staging, which is free. If you are ready to learn the full craft and business, choose The Staging Journey.

Can I turn this into a business?

Yes. The Staging Journey gives you the foundation, and The Home Staging Business Course goes deeper into pricing, profit and planning.

Can I get a refund or pay in instalments?

Courses are digital and open as soon as you buy, so we do not offer refunds or split payments. The free Introduction to Home Staging is the best way to try our teaching first.
