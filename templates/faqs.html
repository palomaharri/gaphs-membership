Frequently asked questions

Information on membership, the platform and member services.

Getting started

How do I log in for the first time?

Use the personal invite link or code you were sent. Choose a password, complete your business page and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy. After that, log in with your email and password.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link will arrive in your inbox. If it does not arrive, please check your spam folder or contact the Association.

My invitation code is not accepted.

Codes are single use and linked to one member. If your code has already been used or is not accepted, please contact the Association and the matter will be resolved.

Which email address should I use?

The one your invite was sent to. To change it later, please contact the Association.

Your membership

Who can join GAPHS?

GAPHS has two membership categories. Members are home staging professionals who have completed a recognised home staging training course, which is declared on application; Members are listed in the global directory. Associates are professionals from related industries, such as real estate agents, property developers and photographers. All members undertake to uphold the Code of Ethics and Conduct.

What are GAPHS chapters?

National chapters represent GAPHS in their country or region and bring members together locally. Please contact the Association for information on existing chapters or on founding a new one.

Does my existing membership carry over?

Yes. Your membership continues on the new platform and renews on your usual renewal date.

When does my membership renew, and at what price?

On your usual renewal date, at the standard rate. You will receive a reminder beforehand.

Can I pay in instalments or get a refund?

Payments are one-off annual payments and are non-refundable, as set out in the Terms.

How do I update my card, invoice details or cancel auto-renewal?

Through the billing area in My account.

Your business page and the Directory

How do I complete my business page?

Log in, go to your profile and fill in your business details, services, website and contact information. A complete page improves visibility to clients.

What can I edit on my page?

Your description, services, areas covered, website, social links and photos.

Who can see my page?

Visitors browsing the Directory. Your private account details are not shown.

How do I appear in the Directory?

Directory pages are for Members (home stagers). Complete and publish your business page and it is listed under your country. Associates do not have a directory page.

Can I change or remove my listing?

Yes, at any time from your profile.

Resources and learning

Where are the member resources?

In the Library. Log in, open Library in the menu and browse by category: courses, video guides, the Atelier Series guides and templates.

How do I find a specific resource?

Use the categories in the Library.

Are the courses included in my membership?

Yes, the courses are included for members. If a course shows "Coming soon", its lessons are still being finished and it will appear when ready.

Can I share resources with non-members?

Members-only material may not be shared with non-members, in accordance with the Terms.

Support and community

Do you offer workshops or meet-ups?

GAPHS runs webinars, and local chapters organise events in their regions. Members are informed in advance.

Is there a forum or community space?

Yes. The members’ Forum is available for questions, professional exchange and networking.

How do I contact GAPHS?

By email at info@gaphs.org.

Technical questions

The website is not working correctly. What should I do?

Please refresh the page, try another browser, or log out and in again. If the problem persists, contact the Association, stating the page and device concerned.

Why am I logged out after a while?

For security reasons, sessions end after a period of inactivity. Please log in again.

I did not receive a confirmation or reminder email.

Please check your spam folder first, then contact the Association.

Further questions? Contact us.
