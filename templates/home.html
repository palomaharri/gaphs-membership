Who we are

A global association for the profession of Home Staging.

GAPHS, the Global Association of Professional Home Stagers, is a non-profit association registered in Switzerland and a global movement of professionals, educators and partners committed to elevating the standards of Home Staging across continents, cultures and markets. GAPHS is a direct member of FIABCI.

We bring together Home Staging professionals, approved training providers and affiliate organisations to promote education, ethical practice and the global recognition of Home Staging as a respected profession and an essential part of the real estate sector.

Our mission

To advance and elevate the profession of Home Staging worldwide by setting and upholding high standards of Education, Ethics and Excellence, and by supporting professionals through certification, collaboration and continued development.

Our vision

For Home Staging to be universally recognised as an essential global profession and an indispensable part of the real estate sector, with GAPHS setting the benchmarks, supporting professionals and shaping policy and public perception.

Our commitment to education

Education is the foundation of professional integrity. Every GAPHS Member has completed formal training in Home Staging, ensuring a consistent benchmark of excellence across the profession. Through education, global collaboration and ongoing support, we equip professionals with the tools, recognition and network they need to succeed in an evolving industry.

What we do

  • Raise the profile and standing of Home Staging globally
  • Review and approve Home Staging training courses
  • Uphold a Code of Ethics that every member follows

Giving back

Worldwide Staging Service Week® (WWSSW®)

A global initiative with a local impact.

Each year, GAPHS members and chapters volunteer their time and Home Staging expertise to support charitable organisations, families and communities that can benefit from their work.

Every project is led by a GAPHS member who serves as its ambassador, and all chapters are invited to take part. Together, these projects give members the opportunity to make a practical contribution, while showing how Home Staging skills can serve communities beyond the property market.

WWSSW® is GAPHS's principal annual service initiative: a shared expression of the profession's commitment to community, collaboration and social impact.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What are the benefits of joining?

GAPHS membership offers access to education, professional exchange and a global network.

  • Explore courses, video guides, Atelier Series guides and practical tools in the GAPHS Library.
  • Take part in webinars and connect with professionals across the GAPHS network.
  • Members and Associates may have a listing in the global directory once their business profile is complete.
  • Members may use the official GAPHS Member logo, participate in their national chapter and its events, and vote at the General Assembly.
Who can join?

The Member category is for Home Staging professionals who have completed a Home Staging training course and meet GAPHS membership requirements.

The Associate category is for professionals in related industries who work with or alongside Home Stagers.

Members and Associates commit to upholding the GAPHS Code of Ethics.

How do I get a directory listing?

Directory listings are available to Members and Associates. Once you have joined GAPHS and completed your business profile, it will appear in the global directory under your country.
