Who we are

One association for home staging, worldwide.

GAPHS, the Global Association of Professional Home Stagers, unites professionals, key partners and affiliates across continents to elevate the Home Staging industry through advocacy, education and collaboration.

We set and uphold high standards of education, ethics and excellence, approve home staging training courses, and support our members with ongoing education, events and recognition. GAPHS is a non-profit association registered in Switzerland, the home staging knowledge partner of CEPI, the European Council of Real Estate Professions, and a direct member of FIABCI, the International Real Estate Federation.

  • Raising the professional image of home staging with the public and the real estate world
  • Approving training courses that meet professional standards
  • A Code of Ethics every member signs

National chapters

GAPHS in your country.

GAPHS chapters bring members together in their own country, in their own language. They organise educational and networking events, promote home staging with local media and real estate agencies, and take part in GAPHS-wide campaigns and conferences.

GAPHS Italia was founded in Rome in April 2026. Every chapter works under the GAPHS umbrella, to the same standards and Code of Ethics.

Start a chapter

Five or more active members in your country can propose a new chapter to the Board. Once approved, a chapter receives:

  • Official recognition and its own chapter logo
  • An announcement on every GAPHS channel
  • Mentoring from the Board and established chapter leaders
  • Promotion of its local and national events

What are the benefits of joining?
A Library of courses, video guides, the Atelier Series guides and practical tools.
A community of professionals in many countries who share what works, plus webinars and local chapters.
Recognition: for Members, a directory listing and use of the GAPHS member logo, so clients and agents can find you and trust you.
Who can join?

Members are home stagers who have completed a home staging training course. Associates are industry partners who work with home stagers. Everyone agrees to the GAPHS Code of Ethics and Conduct.

What is the difference between a Member and an Associate?

Members are trained home stagers: they get a page in the global directory and the GAPHS member logo. Associates are industry partners such as real estate agents, developers and photographers: they join the community and the Library, without a stager listing.

How do I get a directory listing?

Join as a Member and complete your business page. It appears in the directory under your country.

I work in real estate. Is there something for me?

Yes: join as an Associate. You get the course Home Staging for Real Estate Agents, the Library and the community, and the directory helps you find a trained stager for your listings.
